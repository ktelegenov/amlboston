Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how AML Boston LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the Dala app and on amlboston.com.

Information we collect

Dala does not require an account and does not collect, store, or share personal information. Your lesson progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising and tracking

Dala does not show ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you, and we do not share them with anyone.

Children

Dala is suitable for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@amlboston.com
