# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary (main page): anyone checking who AML Boston is: Dala users and App Store review, readers, and research buyers. The main page is a simple company overview; Dala gets its own fuller page later.

Dala page: English speakers learning Kazakh who find Dala, and App Store review, who need a real company site with working Support, Privacy Policy and Terms of Use pages behind the app. They arrive from the App Store listing or a search, check who makes the app, and look for help or the legal pages.

Secondary: parents and young readers (the Tomiris book), and university and research-lab procurement teams (Supply), who need to see a credible vendor they can email for a quote.

## Product Purpose
amlboston.com is the company site of AML Boston LLC, a small Massachusetts studio with three lines of work: Dala: Learn Kazakh (an iPhone app, coming soon), illustrated children's books from Central Asian legends (on hold: the Books page is removed for now and returns later), and supply of robotics components, parts and lab equipment to universities and research institutions. Success: Dala users and App Store review find support and the legal pages in one click; readers and buyers understand the other two lines and know how to get in touch.

## Positioning
A Boston studio rooted in Kazakh and Central Asian culture: it teaches Kazakh in the Latin alphabet, retells steppe legends for children, and supplies research hardware. The site names no supply customers or regions.

## Operating Context
Static site on GitHub Pages (CNAME www.amlboston.com). One contact address for everything: info@amlboston.com. Support replies within 2 business days.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pages: Home, Apps, Supply, Support, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, 404. All must remain.
- Static HTML/CSS, no build step.
- Dala: no account, no data collection, progress stays on device, no ads or tracking; licensed under Apple's Standard EULA. Lessons are bite-sized, with native-speaker audio, Kazakh taught in the Latin alphabet.
- Undecided: Dala launch date, App Store link, book store links, prices.

## Evidence on Hand
No logo, app icon, screenshots, book art or photographs yet. No testimonials, customer names, user numbers or reviews exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. Dala comes first: support and legal pages are always one click away.
2. Say plainly what each line of work is; no inflated claims.
3. One studio, three lines: each gets its own clear place without competing.
4. Rooted in the steppe: the Kazakh and Central Asian thread is real and is what makes the studio distinct.
